Tag guide

*2* … *2*read what's between twice (*3* for three times)
*p1*pause 1 second (*p0.5*, *p3* …)
*d*ding
*dd*ding-dong
*b*beep
*c*chime
A: B:start a line with it to choose the voice. 女： and 男： work too. No label = voice A.

Tags can sit on their own line or at the end of a line, e.g. 问题：…？ *p1* *d*
